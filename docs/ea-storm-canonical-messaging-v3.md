## Canonical Narrative

Your business shouldn't depend on you to keep everything moving.

That's why we built EA STORM, an intelligence platform designed to work across the systems businesses already use to identify what matters, put the right work in the right hands, and turn decisions into accountable action and verified results.

Our inspiration came in part from Storm, the service dog of our Co-Founder and CTO, Monica. Her remarkable ability to recognize a critical signal and respond when Monica needed her showed us the power of recognizing what matters and acting when it matters most.

Because growing your business shouldn't mean carrying more of it yourself.

But our vision goes beyond technology.

What we build matters. What it makes possible matters more.

As a military-spouse-founded technology company, we believe business success should create opportunities beyond the business.

Our mission is to build technology that moves organizations forward while creating opportunities to:

Support military families and expand meaningful remote employment opportunities for military spouses.

Serve individuals with special needs and their families through philanthropic initiatives.

Strengthen marriages and families through our ongoing work with Valor & Vows, a military marriage ministry.

We're building more than software. We're building a company grounded in service, accountability, and purpose.

EA STORM. Keep What Matters Moving.

### Approved Photography Direction

The master direction is `Natural Light. Quiet Confidence.` The final set should
represent multicultural communities authentically across the four themes,
without tokenism, stereotypes, staged charity, or implied beneficiary claims.

- Military-spouse employment: a capable professional working in a calm,
  contemporary environment with natural or window light; no uniforms, staged
  smiles, recruiting cues, or military props.
- Special-needs families: dignity, belonging, and real family connection; no
  pity, clinical framing, staged charity, or implication that the people shown
  have received services from EA STORM.
- Marriages and families: an authentic multicultural military couple or family;
  prefer approved Valor & Vows photography and avoid branch-specific or
  exaggerated military imagery.
- Community and service: military spouses, entrepreneurs, mentors, or
  professionals collaborating naturally; no staged handshakes or recruiting
  imagery.

Every final photograph requires documented usage rights and appropriate model
releases. Images containing identifiable minors require the additional
permissions appropriate to their use.
